# Mastering Director — Audio Mastering Stage

Delivers broadcast-ready, radio-polished stereo masters from vocal audio and instrumental accompaniment.

## Quality Bar
1. **Surgical EQ**:
   - 80Hz high-pass filter cuts sub-bass rumble.
   - 3.5kHz presence peak enhances vocal intelligibility and diction.
   - 10kHz air shelf gives shimmering acoustic brightness.
2. **De-Essing**:
   - Tame harsh sibilance at 6.5kHz with frequency-selective attenuation.
3. **Acoustic Environment**:
   - Apply realistic room or church acoustic space (`church` preset for hymns/choral worship, `hall` for classical).
4. **Sidechain Ducking**:
   - Automatically carve -2.5dB from accompaniment when vocals are actively singing.
5. **Loudness Standards**:
   - Master to integrated -14.0 LUFS (-1.5 dB true peak) matching YouTube and streaming platforms.
